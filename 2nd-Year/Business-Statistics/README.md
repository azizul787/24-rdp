## How exam questions usually come (NU pattern)

The Business Statistics exam paper has **three sections**:

| Section | Type | Marks each | Total |
|---------|------|------------|-------|
| A | Very Short / MCQ / Fill in the blanks | 1 | 20 |
| B | Short Questions (write any 5 out of 8) | 4 | 20 |
| C | Broad Questions / Math problems (write any 5 out of 8) | 12 | 60 |
| | **Total** | | **100** |

So in your exam, you must learn:
- Concepts (for MCQ + short questions)
- Math formulas + solved problems (for broad questions in Section C)
